FROM QUESTION TO RESULT

How data becomes a business decision.

A useful analysis starts with a clear question. Data, assumptions and analytical methods are combined to produce a result that can be reviewed and acted upon.

  1. STEP 01

    Define the question

    Start with a specific assumption. For example: are we paying too much for our current carriers?

  2. STEP 02

    Collect the data

    Gather historical and recent invoices, including transport routes, shipment types and individual cost components.

  3. STEP 03

    Classify past cases

    Identify invoices known to be correct and those that were too expensive. These classifications provide reference examples.

  4. STEP 04

    Analyse patterns

    Statistical methods or a suitable machine-learning model can examine relationships between cost type, transport mode, route and previously classified invoices.

  5. STEP 05

    Review the results

    Flag invoices that may be incorrect. Estimate the amounts requiring investigation and the administrative effort that a more focused review could save.

Analytical results depend on data quality, assumptions and validation. Flagged costs are opportunities for investigation, not automatically confirmed savings.
